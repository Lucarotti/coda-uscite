Coda Uscite

Personal desktop tool that uploads the owner's own short videos as drafts to the owner's own TikTok accounts.

About Terms of Service Privacy Policy

About the app

Desktop · single operator · not offered to the public

Coda Uscite is a small script that runs on its owner's computer. It reads a schedule of finished video edits and, at the planned time, sends each video to the TikTok inbox of one of the owner's accounts as a draft. The owner then opens TikTok, reviews the draft and publishes it by hand.

What it uses from TikTok

  • Login Kit with user.info.basic, to know which of the owner's accounts is connected.
  • Content Posting API with video.upload, to upload a video as a draft to that account's inbox.

The app does not read messages, follow or comment on other accounts, collect data about other users, or publish anything without the owner's review in the TikTok app.

Privacy Policy

Effective 6 November 2026

Data the app handles

  • The access token TikTok issues when the owner connects one of their accounts.
  • Basic profile information of that account (open ID, display name, avatar), used only to show which account is connected.
  • The owner's own video files and captions, sent to TikTok as drafts.

Where it is stored

Tokens and settings stay in a local file on the owner's computer. They are not sent to any server other than TikTok's API.

Sharing

No data is sold, shared with third parties or used for advertising. The app does not collect data about any other TikTok user.

Retention and deletion

Data is kept only while the account stays connected. Disconnecting the app in TikTok, or deleting the local settings file, removes access and the stored token.
